Data Summary for Kd(PAR)_estimation.xlsx

1. File Information
- File name: Kd(PAR)_estimation.xlsx
- Content: Annual summer diffuse attenuation coefficient for photosynthetically active radiation [Kd(PAR)] for Chinese lakes from 2003 to 2024, together with lake attributes and summary statistics.
- Rows: Each row corresponds to one unique lake.
- Columns: Lake metadata, annual Kd(PAR) values, and summary statistics.

2. Variable Description

Lake metadata
- Hylak_id (integer): Unique identifier of the lake.
- HH_zone (string): Location relative to the Hu Huanyong Line (east or west).
- Lake_type (string): Depth group used in the study (shallow lakes or deep lakes).
- Lake_area (float, km^2): Lake surface area.
- Pour_long, Pour_lat (float, decimal degrees): Pour-point longitude and latitude.
- Center_long, Center_lat (float, decimal degrees): Lake-center longitude and latitude.

Annual Kd(PAR), 2003-2024
- 2003 ... 2024 (float, m^-1): Annual summer mean Kd(PAR) retrieved using the Gradient Boosting Tree model.
- Missing cells indicate that no valid annual value was available for that lake-year.

Derived statistics
- Mean (float, m^-1): Arithmetic mean of available annual Kd(PAR) values.
- Sen_rate (float, m^-1 decade^-1): Theil-Sen slope of Kd(PAR) change per decade.

3. Data Structure and Quality Notes
- Number of lake rows: 966.
- Unique Hylak_id values: 966.
- Lakes with at least one annual value and a calculated mean: 916.
- Lakes with a calculated Theil-Sen trend: 909.
- Fifty lakes have no valid annual Kd(PAR) value during 2003-2024.
- Blank cells indicate unavailable values.
